## Hobbies <img src="/images/now/31231a0e.gif" alt="" width="20" height="20" class="kaomoji" />

I started learning Japanese in April 2024. Right now, I know about 450 kanji (meanings and readings). Speaking is my weak point :( whenever I talk to a Japanese person I turn into a stuttering mess. I use [WaniKani](https://www.wanikani.com/) for kanji acquisition, and I try to immerse by watching anime and reading [Tadoku](https://tadoku.org/japanese/en/free-books-en/) books. I take classes at the [University of Amsterdam](https://www.uvatalen.nl/japans), but I just had a six month break. I’m excited to get back into it, though.

I’m practicing ashtanga yoga twice a week, and try to go to the gym for strength training twice a week as well.

I’ve gotten into journaling this year. I have a traveler’s notebook and a Hobonichi. It’s hard not to spend all my money on washi tape, stickers, and new fineliners.
