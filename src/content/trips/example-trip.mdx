## Day 1: Arriving in the City

We landed at Copenhagen Airport and took the metro straight to the city center. The first thing that struck me was how bike-friendly everything is.

## Day 2: Exploring Nyhavn

The colorful houses along the canal are even more beautiful in person than in photos.

## Tips for Visitors

- Get a Copenhagen Card for free public transport
- Try the open-faced sandwiches (smørrebrød)
- Rent a bike to explore like a local
