---
title: "On Digital Gardens"
description: "Some thoughts on why I decided to build this site as a digital garden."
date: 2024-12-30
tags: ["meta", "web"]
---

A digital garden is different from a traditional blog. Instead of polished, finished posts published in reverse chronological order, it's more like a collection of evolving ideas.

Some notes will be short and rough. Others might grow over time as I learn more about a topic.

The beauty of this approach is that it removes the pressure of perfection. It's okay to publish something incomplete and come back to it later.

> The goal is to learn in public and share the journey, not just the destination.
